COURSES OFFERED
- Engineering Degree (B.Tech): Information Technology, Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electronics & Computer Science, Data Science, AI & ML, and First Year Engineering.
- Postgraduate: M.Tech (Computer Engineering), MCA, and MBA/MMS.
- Engineering Diploma: First Year Post-SSC Diploma and Direct Second Year Diploma.
- BBA: Admissions notice for the first year is on the site.
- Honours/Minor degree programs: Available from A.Y. 2022-23 onwards, with a syllabus booklet.

ACCREDITATION AND RANKINGS
- NAAC accreditation with an A+ grade.
- NBA accreditation.
- Recognised in the World Institutional Ranking (WIR "OBE 23").
- Times Engineering Survey 2026: ranked 10th overall among engineering colleges in the MMRDA Region and 5th among engineering colleges in the Western Region of Mumbai.
- Facilities mentioned: modern industry-supported labs, an AICTE IDEA Lab, and multiple Centres of Excellence.

ADMISSIONS (A.Y. 2026-27)
The site lists prospectuses (Engineering/MCA and MBA/BBA) plus admission notifications, schedules and merit lists for:
- First Year B.Tech, with ACAP and IL (Institute Level) seat rounds 1 to 3
- Direct Second Year B.Tech, with ACAP rounds 1 and 2
- First Year M.Tech
- MCA, with rounds 1 to 3
- MBA, with rounds 1 to 3
- BBA
- First Year Diploma and Direct Second Year Diploma
Fee structures are published separately for FY B.Tech, DSE B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA and MCA, under "FRA-Fee Structure" on the website. Exact fee amounts are not included here.
